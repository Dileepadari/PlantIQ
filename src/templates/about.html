About PlantIQ

Detecting plant stress early, from the volatile compounds a plant gives off.

What it does

A plant under attack changes what it emits into the air before it looks unwell. PlantIQ puts a VOC sensor on the plant alongside temperature, humidity, soil moisture, light and CO2, and watches all six together so a pathogen attack shows up while there is still time to act.

Sensors run off an ESP32 on an experimental farm. Readings go to ThingSpeak and to an OM2M middlenode; this site reads them back and compares each one against the safe range for the plant you selected.

The pages

  • Dashboard - current value of every sensor and the VOC trend.
  • Statistics - one chart per sensor over your chosen window.
  • Analysis - each reading against the plant's safe range.
  • History - readings between two dates, exportable as CSV.
  • Alerts - threshold breaches the device reported.
  • Circuit - how the hardware is wired.
  • Settings - which plant to watch and how much history to chart.

Sensors

SensorReadsPrinciple
DHT11 Temperature, humidity A humidity-sensitive polymer and a thermistor, both read as resistance changes.
Capacitive soil probe Soil moisture Dielectric permittivity of the soil rises with water content.
SGP30 CO2, VOC A metal-oxide film changes conductivity in the presence of the target gases.
LDR Light intensity Photoconductivity: resistance falls as incident light rises.
